A. Guided tour on the real screen. A welcome box, then 7 steps. Each step dims the screen except one control and explains it in a bubble:
  1. Recordings and adding files
  2. Play and the waveform
  3. Editing the transcript
  4. Parts on music
  5. Send to main
  6. The main timeline: lanes, volume, fades
  7. Export and Help
Next / Back / Skip tour, arrow keys work. Quick to follow, but you only read, you don't do.

In every option: it starts the first time someone opens Yarnit, with a small demo project (a short interview, a voice-over and a music file) so every step has something real to point at. Skip is always there, Esc skips too, and it never comes back on its own. Help > Take the tour restarts it later. Deleting the demo project is one click.
