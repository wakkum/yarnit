A. Click any spot on a clip: a small menu opens right there with Cut here, Play from here and Clip settings. A dashed line shows the exact spot. Press C to cut at the playhead without the menu. Dragging still moves the clip, so the menu only opens on a click without movement.

Snapping (the same in A, B and C): while you drag a clip, its edges jump to any clip edge on any lane, the playhead and 0:00 when within a few pixels, and a yellow guide line says what it snapped to. A voice clip dragged after another snaps flush against it (no pause). Snap in the play row, or S, turns it off; holding Alt while dragging skips it once.
Cutting (the same in all): the cut makes two clips with the same volume; the first keeps the fade in, the second the fade out. One Undo puts it back together. On a voice clip the cut moves to the nearest gap between words, and the transcript splits into two clip headers.
