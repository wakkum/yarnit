A. A volume line on the selected clip. The music lane grows taller while a clip in it is selected, so the line is easy to grab. Click the line to add a keyframe, drag a dot (up/down = volume, sideways = time), double-click or Delete removes it; a label shows time and volume while dragging. Lower under voice (in the clip settings below) dips the line wherever a voice clip plays: shaded stripes on the clip mark those stretches, and they follow the voice clips when they move or are cut. Keyframes add on top (the swell at the end).

In every option: the line is the clip's own volume over time; the Volume slider and the fades still apply on top (30% volume with a point at 70% plays at 21%). The live player and the export follow the same curve. Lower under voice is off by default; it applies to music and sound effects.
